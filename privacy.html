Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 23, 2026

This privacy policy describes how the Page to B-Roll Chrome Extension handles information when you use our services. We value your privacy and are committed to maintaining full transparency.

1. 100% Local Data Processing

Page to B-Roll is designed to process and store your project data completely on your local machine:

  • All B-roll scenes, text selections, and configurations are stored securely inside the browser's local storage (using the standard chrome.storage.local API) on your device.
  • Video rendering and canvas compositing tasks are executed locally in a background offscreen page within your browser context.

2. No Data Collection or Transmission

We do not collect, monitor, or transmit any user data. Your data never leaves your computer:

  • We do not operate any external data collection, analytic servers, or tracking systems.
  • Your webpage selections, screenshots, and exported files are never uploaded or shared with us or any third parties.

3. Future Payment & Licensing Services

In future versions of this extension, we may introduce payment integrations (via secure third-party billing providers like Stripe or Gumroad) and license key verification:

  • Payments: All transaction details will be handled directly on the payment provider's secure platform. The extension will not collect or store your billing or credit card numbers.
  • Licensing: A license key and email verification request will be securely sent to our license validation endpoint solely to check authorization for Pro features.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or feedback regarding this privacy policy, please contact us at your support email address.
